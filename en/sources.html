Wikipedia and social platforms

Wikidata currently shows no sitelink to a standalone Wikipedia biography in English, Arabic or French, and its own record is sparse. Some broader Wikipedia-derived articles mention Kluiters, but they are not a sufficient biography.

News coverage reproduces photographs from the Barqa Archive; some images are described as having circulated on Facebook. We could not verify an official public Facebook or Instagram account dedicated to Father Nicolas. This site does not attribute accounts to his family, supporters or the cause; it links to the published stories that reproduced the images.

Discrepancies we state openly

  • Sources differ on when his Barqa ministry began; we use “mid-1970s” and explain the difference.
  • We separate the reported kidnapping on 14 March 1985 from the Jesuit report that his body was found on 1 April.
  • We do not announce a cause stage after the diocesan opening unless we find a newer official notice.
  • A press report identifies a tomb at Deir Taanayel; no official confirmation of a transfer of remains or approved shrine was found.
