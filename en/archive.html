Photographic archive

This gallery preserves the project photographs as supplied, adds historical images published and credited to the “Barqa Archive,” and includes a recent photograph of the health centre. Each image has a source and a note on its limits.

The historical images below appear in Nidaa Al Watan’s 2025 feature, which credits a group of them to the Barqa Archive. They are loaded from the newspaper’s image server and linked to the original story; they are not re-uploaded to this repository. Rights remain with the image owners or publisher. Photographer names, dates and licences are welcome additions.

The “project” photographs are the five files supplied with this website. We do not have photographer or date information for each file.

Visual distinctions: the colorized portrait is a digital restoration, not an original color photograph. The memorial artwork is contemporary, not a historical scene. No AI-generated image is presented as archival photography.
