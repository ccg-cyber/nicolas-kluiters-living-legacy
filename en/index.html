A continuing local legacy

Barqa’s community health centre today

The Lebanese Association of the Order of Malta says the Barqa centre was established in 1985 in memory of Father Nicolas and is operated with the Sisters of Jesus and Mary. It is an institution established after his death; we do not say he personally founded it.
