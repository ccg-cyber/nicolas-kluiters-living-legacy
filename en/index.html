Documented biography · Barqa’s living memory

A life of faith and service in the Bekaa

Father Nicolas Kluiters was a Dutch Jesuit, an artist before priesthood, and a pastor and social worker among villages in Lebanon’s northern Bekaa. This archive brings together the documented story, the practical legacy, and the memory that remains in Barqa.

A continuing local legacy

Barqa’s community health centre today

The Lebanese Association of the Order of Malta says the Barqa centre was established in 1985 in memory of Father Nicolas and is operated with the Sisters of Jesus and Mary. It is an institution established after his death; we do not say he personally founded it.
