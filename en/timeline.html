Documented milestones and stated discrepancies

Timeline

This timeline includes events with published support. Where sources differ, it gives a range or explains the disagreement instead of claiming false precision.

  1. 25 May 1940

    Born in Delft

    Nicolas Kluiters was born in Delft, the Netherlands. Dutch Jesuit biography

  2. 1966

    Arrival in Lebanon

    Press sources date his arrival in Lebanon to this year, following fine-arts studies and the beginning of Jesuit formation. He studied Arabic and social work in Beirut. L’Orient-Le Jour

  3. Summer 1973

    Ordained a priest

    He was ordained in Amsterdam, according to the Dutch Jesuit biography. Dutch Jesuits

  4. Mid-1970s

    Pastoral ministry in the northern Bekaa

    Published sources give different starting years for his ministry in Barqa: 1974, 1976 and 1977, and one account cites a later appointment. Jesuit sources agree that he served Bekaa villages for about ten years. We do not choose one year without qualification. Jesuits in Europe

  5. c. 1980

    Live Stations of the Cross

    Jesuit accounts describe an annual live Stations of the Cross with Barqa residents from around 1980. Jesuit retrospective

  6. 14 March 1985

    Kidnapped

    Father Nicolas was kidnapped in the Bekaa during the Lebanese Civil War. The sources reviewed here do not establish one perpetrator in a court-confirmed account. Jesuits in Europe

  7. 1 April 1985

    His body was found

    The Jesuit account reports that his body was found in a deep ravine. A later press article identifies his tomb at Deir Taanayel. Jesuits in Europe · Nidaa Al Watan

  8. 1985

    Barqa health centre established in his memory

    The Lebanese Association of the Order of Malta dates the centre’s establishment to 1985, after his death. Association Malte Liban

  9. 22 January 2022

    Diocesan inquiry opened

    Bishop César Essayan opened the diocesan phase of the beatification cause in Beirut. This is a step in a Church process, not a beatification. Jesuit announcement

Key dates are based on published sources. The exact start of his Barqa appointment, details of his final day and any cause developments after the inquiry opened require additional primary documentation.
