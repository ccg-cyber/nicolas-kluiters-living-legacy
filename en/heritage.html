Maronite heritage

A Church centuries old: saints, feasts, the Aramaic heritage and the cedars.

01

A Church born of prayer

The Maronite Church is an Eastern Catholic Church of the Syriac-Antiochene tradition, in full communion with the Bishop of Rome, with its patriarchal seat at Bkerké. It takes its name from Saint Maroun, a Syrian hermit of the late 4th and early 5th century. In Lebanon it lives alongside other Christian churches and Muslim neighbours.

Father Nicolas Kluiters was a Dutch Jesuit of the Latin Church who served in Lebanon, where Maronites are the largest Catholic community. This page offers context for the faith lived around him; it is not an official site of the Maronite Church.
