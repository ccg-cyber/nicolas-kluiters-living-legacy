A moment of peace

Pause. Listen. Serve.

Holy Spirit, fill our hearts with your peace. Teach us to listen, to forgive, and to serve. Strengthen those who care for their neighbours, and bring hope to the people of Barqa and all Lebanon. Through Christ our Lord. Amen.
